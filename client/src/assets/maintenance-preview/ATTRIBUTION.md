# Bus model used in the local maintenance preview

3D Bus by ajanhallinta, published December 7, 2017 under CC0.
Source: https://opengameart.org/content/3d-bus
Archive: https://opengameart.org/sites/default/files/3DBus_by_ajanhallinta.zip

bus.obj and bus.mtl are copied from the archive's OBJ directory. The preview
normalizes the model's scale and changes materials at runtime. Original model
contains a body and four wheels, not mechanical internals. Repair markers are
illustrative, editable overlays, not manufacturer-verified component locations.
